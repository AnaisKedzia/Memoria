What are flashcards ?

📝

Flashcards are part of a system called spaced repetition, designed to improve retention of information and long term memory



Flashcards have questions and answers. They are reviewed on a regular basis, right before the information is forgotten.

Planting the seeds of knowledge

How to use our flashcards ?

1.

Create deck

🗳️

Decks are containers for cards, it can be divided by subject. A deck is necessary to start creating cards.

2.

Create cards

✍

Cards have a front and back. On the back is the piece of information you want to memorize. The front should have a keyword or a question.

3.

Start review

📚

Test yourself on what you remember, each card have a unique review date that is adjusted after each review.

Cards maturity

New

Cards you haven't been exposed to yet

Familiar

Cards you are still in the process of learning. If neglected, they can be forgotten

Mature

Cards with a high rentention rate and will likely not be forgotten
